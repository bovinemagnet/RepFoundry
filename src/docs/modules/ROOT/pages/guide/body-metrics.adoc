= Body metrics
Paul Snow
0.0.0
:description: How to record and track body weight and body fat percentage in RepFoundry.

RepFoundry lets you record body weight over time, alongside an optional body
fat percentage and a note, so you can track how your body is changing outside
of the numbers on the bar.

== What you can record

Each entry captures:

* *Weight* — required, shown in whichever unit (kg or lbs) you've set for the
  app.
* *Body fat percentage* — optional.
* *Notes* — an optional free-text note.
* *Date* — defaults to today, but can be changed.

There is no field for other body measurements such as waist or chest — only
weight, body fat percentage, and notes are recorded.

== Adding an entry

From the *Body Metrics* screen, tap the *Add Measurement* button. Enter a
weight, and optionally a body fat percentage and a note, then save. The entry
is added to your history immediately.

== Viewing your history

The screen shows your most recent entry first, as a summary card with your
latest weight and body fat percentage (if you recorded one). Below that, once
you have at least two entries, a chart plots your weight over time so you can
see the trend at a glance.

Your full history is listed underneath, most recent first, showing the date,
weight, body fat percentage and notes for each entry. Swipe an entry to
delete it.

== Importing from your health app

If xref:guide/health-sync.adoc[Health Sync] is turned on and allowed to read
weight data, RepFoundry checks your phone's health store for a weight reading
newer than your most recent entry. When it finds one, it offers to import it
as a new entry with a single tap.

If Health Sync is allowed to write data instead, saving a new body metrics
entry automatically sends the weight — and body fat percentage, if you
entered one — to your phone's health store.

== Where to find it

Open *Settings* and choose *Body Metrics*.
