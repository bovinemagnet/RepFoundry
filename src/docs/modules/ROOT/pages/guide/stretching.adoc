= Stretching
Paul Snow
0.0.0
:description: How to log timed or manual stretching entries against a workout in RepFoundry, using the built-in stretch presets or a custom stretch name.

RepFoundry can record stretching alongside a workout — mobility work, a warm-up, or a cool-down — without it affecting your strength sets. Stretching entries are stored separately from working sets, so they never count towards volume totals or personal records.

There is no dedicated stretching tab. Stretching lives inside an active workout, as a *Stretching* section on the Workout screen.

== Where to find it

The *Stretching* section appears on the active workout screen in two places: above the exercise list while the workout is still empty, and near the bottom, just before the *Finish Workout?* button, once you have logged exercises. Either way, it is part of the same active workout — not a separate screen.

You can reach it in two ways:

* While a workout is already in progress, scroll to the *Stretching* section and tap *Add Stretching* (or *Add*, once you already have entries).
* From the idle Workout tab, before starting a workout, tap *Start Stretching* under "Or start from". This begins a new, empty workout and opens the Add Stretching sheet immediately — it is a shortcut into the same feature, not a different kind of session.

== Stretch presets

The Add Stretching sheet offers twenty built-in stretches as selectable chips:

* Standing Hamstring Stretch
* Seated Forward Fold
* Standing Quadriceps Stretch
* Low Lunge Hip Flexor
* Pigeon Pose
* Butterfly Stretch
* Child's Pose
* Cobra Stretch
* Cat–Cow
* Downward-Facing Dog
* Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch
* Doorway Chest Stretch
* Standing Calf Stretch
* Supine Spinal Twist
* Neck Side Stretch
* Figure-4 Glute Stretch
* 90/90 Hip Stretch
* Frog Pose
* Front Splits
* Side Splits (Middle Splits)

If none of these fit, choose *Custom…* and type your own stretch name (up to 60 characters).

== The timer

The sheet offers three ways to record how long you held a stretch, switched with a segmented control:

* *Timer* — starts a running stopwatch. Tap *Start* to begin, *Pause* to stop counting without losing the elapsed time, and *Reset* to clear it back to zero. The elapsed time is shown as it counts up.
* *Manual* — type minutes and seconds directly, or tap a quick-add chip (1, 2, 5, 10, or 15 minutes) to fill them in for you.
* *Untimed* — just logs that you did the stretch, with no duration recorded. Untimed entries are excluded from the total-minutes count shown on the section.

Pick a preset (or a custom name), choose a recording method, optionally add a note, then tap *Save* to add the entry to the workout. Tapping *Cancel* discards it. If you leave the sheet with a timer running, the elapsed time is kept, so reopening *Add Stretching* picks up where you left off.

== Recording and reviewing entries

Saved stretching entries appear as a list in the *Stretching* section for the rest of the workout, each showing its name, duration (or a dash for untimed entries), and any note. The section header totals the entry count and the combined minutes across all timed entries in the workout.

Once the workout is finished, the same stretching entries appear on that workout's page in xref:guide/history.adoc[], listed with their durations and a combined minutes total.

== Removing an entry

Tap the close icon (an "x") on a stretching entry to remove it. You are asked to confirm before it is deleted.
