:description: RepFoundry is an offline-first workout tracker for the gym floor. Start here to install the app and log your first workout.

RepFoundry is a workout tracker built for the gym floor. It works fully offline, so a patchy signal in the basement of your gym is never a problem, and there is no account to create or sign into before you can start logging a set.

== Who this is for

RepFoundry serves two kinds of user:

Training on your own::
Log workouts, track cardio sessions, watch your heart rate live during a session, and see your progress build up over weeks and months.

Coaching others::
A personal trainer can manage multiple clients from the same app — see xref:guide/clients.adoc[] — following their programmes and reviewing their progress alongside their own training.
